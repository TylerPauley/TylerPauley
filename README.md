### Hi there, I'm Tyler 👋

Hello! I'm Tyler, a curious and self-starting tech enthusiast with a background in cybersecurity and a current role as a Database Coordinator. I'm driven by a passion for making things work better, whether that involves securing a system, automating a tedious task, or coding a creative solution.

With a B.A.S. in Cybersecurity (graduating *Summa cum laude* with a 3.97 GPA) and certifications including **ISC2's Certified in Cybersecurity**, **CompTIA Security+**, and **SecurityX**, I'm now looking to transition into a hands-on SOC Analyst or Cybersecurity Engineering role to further build my experience in the field.

For instance, I developed **FinSync Automator**, an internal utility that reduced a two-hour weekly financial reporting process down to just five minutes. Python is my go-to for scripting and automation, but I'm currently diving into Rust to sharpen my systems-level programming skills. I thrive on technical challenges and am always exploring what's possible with code.
